# V3 scenario onset contract — proposed revision 1

Status: exact proposed synthetic reference conditions for approval. The constants below are design assumptions, not empirically optimized cutoffs, legal thresholds, or deployed detection rules. They apply uniformly to all observable streams as specified in [the ground-truth contract](V3_GROUND_TRUTH_CONTRACT.md).

## Shared event and history semantics

All times are UTC. Windows `(t-L, t]` exclude the lower time boundary and include T and strictly earlier records at the upper time boundary; records at the same timestamp with larger transaction IDs are future. Baseline windows never include T. Monetary calculations use integer USD minor units, converted only for stated dollar comparisons. All sums and counts count each actual event once within a subject's history. No artificial history outside the 100,000 transactions exists.

For structuring, the current subject w is the serviced wallet for Cash-In/Cash-Out and the sender for W2W. Wallet activity includes that wallet's cash events and outgoing W2W events. Receiving a W2W does not also count as outgoing structuring activity. An agent service event has a nonempty agent ID and one explicitly recorded serviced wallet; Cash-In, Cash-Out and assisted W2W count. Direct W2W has no agent service event. W2W transfers never change agent float. Cash-Out adds agent float; Cash-In reduces it.

For an L-duration recent window, `B_L = (t-L-28 days, t-L]` is its disjoint prior baseline. `expected_count_L = baseline_count × L/(28 days)`. Rate comparisons divide by `max(1, expected_count_L)`. Baseline eligibility requires a fully observed 28-day baseline and at least 4 wallet events for wallet rate conditions, or 12 service events for agent rate conditions. Observation coverage begins at the entity's first observable event in the partition, never a private activation timestamp; the baseline's lower boundary must be no earlier than that first observation. Wallet coverage can begin with either endpoint of W2W or the wallet's cash event; an agent's begins with its first service. Additional family-specific minima below also apply. Ineligible baseline means that predicate is false; it does not suppress a different eligible family. Missing history is not interpreted as normal baseline rate zero.

For agent A1, compare the current 7-day unique-wallet set with each of the four preceding disjoint 7-day baseline buckets, and use the median of those four distinct-wallet counts. Novelty means absence from their 28-day union. For A2, compare the current 24-hour share with the pooled preceding 28-day share; this sample-size difference must be disclosed and audited. For S3, the recent window is the current UTC day up to T and the baseline is the preceding 28 complete UTC days, including days with zero activity. Calendar-day buckets are the explicit boundary exception: `[midnight, next midnight)` includes midnight, and the current day's bucket stops at T's key. No current-day future transaction enters its count.

All current events must satisfy the subject/type gate stated below. The first qualifying event is onset; subsequent events are positive only while their own condition holds. Threshold equality passes. A denominator of zero fails its condition; it never produces infinity.

## Structuring

| Family | Exact current-event condition, including observed history | Onset and post-onset behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| S1 `variable_fragment_burst` | On w's last 1h including T: at least 4 events, at least 2 distinct amounts, sum ≥ USD 300, largest amount ≤ 60% of that sum, and count ≥ 3 × `max(1, baseline_count/672)`. Wallet baseline eligibility applies for L=1h. | First fragment alone fails. The fourth or a later event can qualify. Expiry or value dominance can make later events 0. |
| S2 `similar_amount_repetition` | On w's last 7d including T, define cluster K by amounts within 5% of **T's** positive amount. Require at least 4 members, cluster sum ≥ USD 200, cluster count / all recent count ≥ 0.60, and cluster count ≥ 2 × `max(1, matching_baseline_count/4)`. Matching baseline amounts use the same current anchor; wallet baseline eligibility applies. | A current amount matching accumulated repetition can trigger. An unrelated current amount is evaluated against its own anchor and does not inherit an earlier cluster alert. |
| S3 `distributed_same_day_fragmentation` | Current UTC-day activity of w including T has at least 6 events across at least 3 distinct UTC hour buckets, sum ≥ USD 500, and count ≥ 2 × `max(1, median daily count in the prior 28 complete days)`. Require at least 4 baseline events and a full 28-day baseline. | Evidence must accumulate across observed hours. No full-day or future-day total may be used. Reset at UTC midnight; a later day must qualify again. |
| S4 `variable_near_threshold_history` | T's amount is in the inclusive USD 7,000–10,500 band around the **synthetic research reference** USD 10,000. In w's last 7d including T: at least 5 events, at least 3 in-band values, at least 2 distinct in-band amounts, in-band share ≥ 0.40, and in-band count ≥ 2 × `max(1, baseline_in_band_count/4)`. Wallet baseline eligibility applies. | An isolated in-band amount fails. Only a current in-band member of accumulated evidence triggers this family; other events can still trigger another family. |

The USD 10,000 reference has no asserted Zimbabwean regulatory meaning. Low and high amounts remain possible in all generation cohorts. The thresholds above define the synthetic alert task, not private campaign intent.

## Wallet and network behaviour

| Family | Exact current-event condition | Onset and post-onset behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| N1 `many_to_one_collection` | T is W2W into receiver w. In prior/current W2W inflows to w during 24h: at least 4 transfers, at least 3 distinct senders, total ≥ USD 300, and transfer count ≥ 2 × `max(1, baseline_inflow_count/28)`. Baseline is the preceding 28d, with at least 4 prior W2W inflows and full coverage. | Current contributor can establish collection now. Earlier cash funding and a first isolated contribution are not N1 positives. Current inflows remain eligible only while the condition holds. |
| N2 `one_to_many_dispersion` | T is W2W from sender w. In prior/current W2W outflows during 24h: at least 4 transfers, at least 3 distinct receivers, total ≥ USD 300, and count ≥ 2 × `max(1, baseline_outflow_count/28)`. Require at least 4 baseline W2W outflows and full coverage. | Current payout can establish dispersion. Funding events are not N2 positives; later unrelated activity does not inherit the label. |
| N3 `reciprocal_relationship_cycle` | T is W2W `s→r`, s≠r. A simple directed path `r→…→s` of 1–5 edges exists in **strictly prior** W2W history, with distinct path nodes, strictly increasing event keys along the path, and every edge in the time window `(t-7d,t]`. T completes a 2–6-wallet cycle. Adjacent prior path amounts have ratios in [0.25,4]; T's amount / the first path-edge amount is in [0.25,1.25]. At least one qualifying path suffices. | Onset is a qualifying current closure. No completed cycle is required before T. Earlier open-path edges are not retroactively positive. Later edges must close their own qualifying paths. Assisted and direct W2W both count; cash events never form graph edges. |
| N4 `wallet_pass_through` | T is an outgoing W2W or Cash-Out of w. Find the earliest strictly prior W2W inflow to w in the time window `(t-24h,t]` by canonical key, including smaller-ID inflows at timestamp t; call its key τ. F is all observed W2W inflow plus Cash-In to w from τ through the instant before T. O is all W2W outflow plus Cash-Out from w strictly after τ, including T. Require F ≥ USD 100, `0.50 ≤ O/F ≤ 1.00`, and T's outflow no greater than `F − O_before_T`. | A current outward leg can trigger using earlier funding. Cash funding and first inbound transfers do not trigger N4. Recompute τ and F/O on each event; no future onward fraction or permanent funding flag is used. |

For N3, exact graph matching and monetary constraints are reference-evaluator duties. A graph feature may summarize prior paths without receiving the truth evaluator's qualifying-path flag. For N4 the reference is a temporal funding proxy, not a claim to trace legally identifiable units of money. Opening wallet balances and unrelated inflows make source attribution inherently limited. Independent accounting must still reject negative wallet balances and agent float.

## Agent behaviour

| Family | Exact current-event condition | Onset and post-onset behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| A1 `agent_wallet_growth` | T is service at agent a. In the current 7d including T: at least 6 distinct serviced wallets; at least 4 absent from the preceding baseline's 28-day union; novel-wallet share ≥ 0.50; and recent distinct count ≥ 1.5 × `max(1, median distinct count of the four preceding 7d buckets)`. Agent baseline eligibility applies. | Several causally observed arrivals and a prior comparator are required. Private plans for future customers are irrelevant. A direct transfer has no A1 subject. |
| A2 `agent_wallet_concentration` | T is service at a. Recent 24h has at least 8 service events. Let C be the fraction served to the two most frequent recent wallets; let B be the corresponding top-two share of the pooled preceding 28d, each set ranked separately. Require `C ≥ 0.65` and `C−B ≥ 0.20`. Baseline must have at least 20 service events, at least 5 distinct wallets, and full 28d coverage. | Accumulated concentration and change must both exist. One wallet's first service is insufficient. Added ordinary customers can end the alert state. |
| A3 `agent_temporal_burst` | T is service at a. Recent 1h including T contains at least 5 service events across at least 3 serviced wallets, and count ≥ 3 × `max(1, baseline_service_count/672)`. Agent baseline eligibility applies. | A current event crossing the intensity condition starts the alert. Rate uses the agent's disjoint prior baseline, never final burst duration or a planned cluster ID. |
| A4 `agent_flow_imbalance` | T is Cash-In or Cash-Out at a. Recent 24h including T has at least 6 cash events across at least 3 wallets. Define `v=(CashOutValue−CashInValue)/(CashOutValue+CashInValue)` and `c=(CashOutCount−CashInCount)/(CashOutCount+CashInCount)`. Require `abs(v) ≥ 0.60`, `abs(c) ≥ 0.50`, matching nonzero signs, and `abs(v−v_baseline) ≥ 0.40`. Baseline is preceding 28d with at least 8 cash events, at least 12 total services and full coverage. | Current cash activity can establish or end accumulated imbalance. Assisted W2W has no cash-flow contribution and cannot trigger A4. Later balancing is evaluated when observed, not anticipated. |

## Strength, short histories, and onset witnesses

Weak, moderate and strong refer to pre-sampled temporal coordination, relationship reuse, and amount variability from shared support, with proportions 20/50/30 by campaign count over the full design. They do not alter the predicates or authorize a positive before onset. A weak campaign may produce no positive; a strong campaign may fail because the comparator is already active. Keep these outcomes.

The family witness ledger records the current subject, qualifying earlier/current event IDs, eligible baseline interval and the predicate version. For a campaign starting with an already active independent condition, record that pre-existing state rather than inventing a fresh onset. Histories are never restricted to known campaign members: ordinary and other-campaign events can strengthen or weaken the observable condition.

Boundary examples to freeze with a future implementation: first three otherwise qualifying S1 fragments are 0 and the fourth can be 1; `a→b, b→c, c→a` can label the closing third edge for N3 if value/time constraints hold, while the first two have no N3 witness; an N4 first inbound leg is 0 for N4 while a later qualifying payout can be 1; W2W assistance changes no A4 float. Existing independent predicates may make any of these events positive for a different family.
