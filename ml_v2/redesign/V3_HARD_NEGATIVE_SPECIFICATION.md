# V3 legitimate hard-scenario specification — proposed revision 1

Retain difficult normal behaviour. H1–H8 name legitimate-origin scenario cohorts; only their events whose universal decision target is 0 are hard negatives in classifier evaluation. A lawful narrative cannot overrule the same observable predicate applied to a suspicious campaign. This distinction prevents deliberately contradictory labels for identical permitted information.

## Family design

| Cohort | Legitimate explanation / matched subtype | Observable distinctions that can keep target 0 | Availability and ambiguity |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Busy merchant days and scheduled burst payments; equally S1/S3 | Repeated ordinary busy periods establish a high prior wallet rate; current bursts may have less joint fragmentation, higher dominant-payment share, or insufficient observed hour spread. | All distinctions require actual prior/current records, not a private merchant flag. An unusual legitimate burst crossing S1/S3 becomes a review target. |
| H2 | Recurring subscriptions, supplier and community collections; S2 | Similar payments already occur in prior weeks, so recurrence need not be a new increase; amount diversity can dilute the current-anchor cluster. | Prior same-anchor frequency and observed mixture are causal. Perfectly matching increased repetition receives the same alert target regardless of purpose. |
| H3 | Recurring inventory or fee payments near the synthetic band; S4 | Comparable in-band activity already exists in the prior baseline, or too few current observations have accumulated. | Existing band values and baseline frequency are visible. No “legitimate large payment” exemption or regulatory claim is introduced. |
| H4 | Merchant/community collection from several sources; N1 | Stable prior receiver activity makes current source collection unsurprising, or current count/value remains below the joint evidence condition. | Genuine receiver history must be generated. New legitimate fundraising can still warrant review under the reference. |
| H5 | Payroll dispersion and treasury/settlement transit; equally N2/N4 | Prior normal payout activity can explain N2 rate; for N4, slower outward movement, incomplete within-window turnover, or insufficient prior transfer funding can leave the condition false. | Distinctions are available when the relevant outflow occurs. Fast legitimate transit that meets N4 is target 1; hidden payroll intent does not make it 0. |
| H6 | Refunds and reciprocal business settlement; N3 | Real reciprocal edges can remain non-qualifying because temporal order or return-value relationships fail the joint cycle condition; other examples provide open paths that may close later. | Do not eliminate legitimate cycles. Qualifying current cycle closures receive target 1 even in H6. Earlier open-path events remain genuine difficult negatives. |
| H7 | Paydays, market growth and temporary queues at agents; equally A1/A3 | Earlier service populations and bursts may already support the current level; arrivals may be mostly returning clients, or a spike may have insufficient observed intensity/population change. | Baselines must be observable in preceding service records. Some legitimate new growth and strong queues remain review positives. |
| H8 | Agents serving a few established businesses and temporary cash demand; equally A2/A4 | Comparable preceding concentration or float imbalance can explain the current level; observed rebalancing may keep joint count/value/change criteria false. | Prior service and cash facts supply the distinction. Hidden business relationships or liquidity explanations cannot override a satisfied predicate. |

These are generation goals for observable histories, not guarantees of target 0 and not directions to search for examples that fool a model. Routine and other episodes remain interleaved. Both campaign and legitimate subjects receive legitimate baselines; private entities are never permanently designated normal or suspicious.

## Controls for generator artefacts

Freeze the following controls by **each matched subtype**: S1/H1-S1, S2/H2, S3/H1-S3, S4/H3, N1/H4, N2/H5-N2, N3/H6, N4/H5-N4, A1/H7-A1, A2/H8-A2, A3/H7-A3, A4/H8-A4. The same recipe must operate in all partitions. Do not diagnose subtype differences by comparing a narrow family against a mixed hard cohort.

| Quantity | Pre-generation control | Model-free acceptance/reporting check |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction type | Shared subtype quota recipe: structuring 35% Cash-In / 35% Cash-Out / 30% W2W; network 10% / 10% / 80%; agent 35% / 35% / 30%. Apply identical deterministic integer remainder allocation to each matched cohort. | Compare proportions by cohort, strength mixture, and partition. Absolute type-rate difference ≤ 1 percentage point after planned rounding; report actual counts. Exact role order can vary only where it is intended behaviour. |
| Channel and agent mediation | Within W2W, mediated fraction 40% for both members of structuring/network pairs; all agent-family W2W is mediated. Cash always uses an agent. Serviced endpoint for assisted W2W follows the same shared rule in each pair. | Conditional channel proportions differ ≤ 1 point, allowing one-event rounding in small strata. Check the rule by type rather than pooling cash and W2W. |
| Episode size | Shared 12/20/28 length mixtures from the dataset specification, including exact partition count recipes. | Exact matched length proportions after declared rounding. No shorter positive campaigns solely for budget packing. Report both generation-cohort and decision-target conditional size summaries. |
| Amount support and formatting | Same base mixture, limits, rounding and decimal precision in matched pairs. Joint S2/S4/N3/N4 relationships may differ as declared behaviour. | Zero out-of-support values, equal formatting rules; report quantiles and boundary masses. Investigate difference in unrelated marginal amount patterns rather than forcing equal behavioural amounts. |
| Duration | Same subtype support and base duration sampling, including continuation branches. Do not change the duration sampler by target. | Report medians/quantiles and empirical CDF distance by generation cohort. CDF distance > 0.20 or absolute standardized mean difference > 0.25 is a review flag, not an instruction to redraw data. |
| Entity activity and exposure | Shared activity-tier and agent-mediation assignment probabilities; matching of baseline eligibility opportunities, with actual differences in prior behaviour permitted where the onset condition requires them. | Report baseline counts, history coverage, tiers and never-eligible rates by subtype/cohort. Unexplained difference > 10 points in baseline eligibility is a review flag. Private tier cannot enter X. |
| Serviced-wallet counts | Same endpoint-service rules and opportunities, conditional on episode length, transaction types, mediation and distinct graph nodes. | Specifically inspect N3/H6 unique-serviced-wallet counts after conditioning on these quantities. Unexplained residual separation must be documented as an artefact; do not expose it as a class-specific constructor flag. |

CDF and standardized-difference cutoffs are preregistered audit flags, not statistical proof of equivalence. Small groups can exceed them through sampling noise. Mechanically enforced schema/quota/isolation violations block release. Statistical flags require a written structural explanation before release; they do not authorize rerolling a seed, dropping episodes or targeting a better model score. An unresolved material construction defect requires a newly versioned generation specification.

The intended positive target is a function of observable behaviour, so conditioning on that target will often create legitimate marginal differences. Balance the **generation recipes and irrelevant opportunities** across matched scenario-origin cohorts. Report target-conditioned differences separately, and classify them as intended evidence, conditioning effects, or unexplained artefacts. Do not force identical feature/amount distributions between target classes, which would erase the defined task.

## Specific response to Stage 7

Stage 7 found N3 Validation contained 150/150 W2W events while H6 included 138 cash events among 825; direct-wallet shares also differed. Its completed-episode serviced-wallet count separated N3/H6 strongly, and episode-size separation changed between Train and Validation. Shared type/channel/length recipes and service-endpoint logic remove those intentional constructor differences from the proposal. They do not guarantee identical realized samples or perfect causal separation.

H1 and H7 remain required challenges. Their frozen V2 FPRs, 0.4303 and 0.3321, justify retaining stable legitimate high activity and newly rising legitimate activity. The latter can be a justified reference review positive; this is distinct from a classifier false positive. Report rates for both, including the proportion of legitimate-origin events that satisfy each oracle family.

No current dataset is changed, and no synthetic legitimate narrative is used as a model feature.
