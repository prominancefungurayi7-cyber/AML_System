Corrections to the submitted chapter drafts:

- Original [17] incorrectly named P. Ncube as co-author. The journal names **E. Torerai**.
- Original [18] incorrectly named P. Ncube and the *Journal of Comparative Law in Africa*. The publication appears under **H. Chitimira and E. Torerai in De Jure Law Journal (2023)**.
- The 2015 and 2011 references exceeded the guide's ten-year limit and were removed. Current source material on graph AML and official XGBoost documentation supports the relevant discussion.
- The 30-feature Stage14 Gradient Boosting results, 0.35 threshold, superseded 2015-era model narrative and unfilled figure placeholders were removed. The replacement reports the actual frozen V4 40-semantic/42-estimator-column contract, 0.50 threshold, synthetic Validation metrics, and recorded limitations.
- No independent Final Test, operational AML detection rate, cross-instance message benchmark, or panel execution time was invented. The timings in Chapter 1 are criteria to measure during the defence.
